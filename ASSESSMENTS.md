## Assessment progression

Assignment 1 establishes the project scope, schedule, risks, acceptance criteria, team practices, and prototype. Assignment 2 implements the raw-data-to-model pipeline for that same topic. Assignment 3 is intended to package the trained model inside a tested, usable React/FastAPI application with data visualisation.

## Course-wide requirements

- All three assignments are teamwork tasks.
- One student submits the group materials for Assignments 1 and 2.
- The unit requires at least 50% overall and at least 40% on each assignment.
- Assignment pages contain legacy wording against generative AI use. The current course clarification recorded on 9 September 2026 permits AI; the older wording has not yet been updated.
- Harvard referencing is required.
- No quiz is visible on the COS30049 Assignments page or Modules page; no quiz was opened or started.
