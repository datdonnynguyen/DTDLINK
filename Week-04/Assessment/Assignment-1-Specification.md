## Purpose

This is the first phase of the three-assignment project. The group selects one of four cybersecurity topics and produces a professional project-management plan and interface prototype that will guide the later machine-learning and website work.

Topics: Prompt Injection Guardrail; AI-Generated Content Detection; Phishing URL Detection; Log Anomaly Detection.

## Required deliverables

1. Comprehensive project-management plan, 800–6,000 words excluding references:
   - background and introduction
   - team introduction
   - requirements list and description
   - scope, WBS, and WBS Dictionary
   - Gantt chart, schedule, dependencies, and critical path
   - risk register and mitigation plan
   - monitoring/change-control plan
   - closure and acceptance criteria
   - front-end prototype and explanation of its usability principles
2. Minutes for at least one team meeting per week since team formation.
3. Signed group contribution form agreed by all members.
4. Harvard-style in-text citations and reference list for external material, including adapted tables and diagrams.

Do not include unnecessary files, do not submit by email, and do not publicly post reasoning or solution material. The assignment page still displays an older prohibition on generative AI; under the current course clarification recorded above, that prohibition is outdated and AI use is permitted.
