## Topic

Project scope, time, and communication management, with practical use of a Work Breakdown Structure (WBS), WBS Dictionary, Gantt chart, and meeting minutes.

## What I should learn

- Define and control project scope and prevent scope creep.
- Collect and document functional and non-functional requirements.
- Create a hierarchical WBS and supporting WBS Dictionary.
- Identify activities, dependencies, milestones, and schedules in a Gantt chart.
- Record decisions, responsibilities, and action items in effective meeting minutes.
- Apply these artefacts to the Assignment 1 project-management plan.
