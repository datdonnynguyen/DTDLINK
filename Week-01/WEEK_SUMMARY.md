## Topic

Unit orientation; project-management foundations; project selection and initiation; business cases; strategic alignment; requirements and prototyping; team formation and cybersecurity project-topic selection.

## What I should learn

- Understand the unit's three-part progression: project management (Weeks 1-3), machine learning (Weeks 4-7), and web development (Weeks 8-12).
- Explain the project-management lifecycle and the role of initiation, planning, monitoring/control, and closing.
- Define project scope, business value, functional and non-functional requirements, risk, strategic alignment, and a business case.
- Use SWOT analysis and prototyping to clarify an idea.
- Form a three-person group, nominate a leader, and select a cybersecurity project topic that will continue through Assignments 1-3.
